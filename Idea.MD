# Project Idea: Human-Backed Agent Invoices

## Goal 

To create a system where human-backed AI agents can issue verified on-chain invoices, using ENS domains as their representation. This aims to provide an automated yet trustworthy method for creating financial documents via AI agents acting on behalf of real individuals.

---

## Key Components

1.  **Owner (with World ID):**
    *   Possesses a unique, verified identity through World ID.
    *   Does **not** directly own the root ENS domain but delegates this capability to their agent.

2.  **Human-Backed Agent:**
    *   An AI agent registered in AgentBook (World ID), cryptographically confirming it represents a unique real individual (the Owner).
    *   Acts on behalf of the Owner.
    *   **Registers the root ENS domain** (e.g., `billie.eth`) to their own wallet, avoiding the need for `setApprovalForAll` and direct Owner-Agent wallet linkage.
    *   Utilizes native tokens for gas fees to create domains.

3.  **Verification Service (Your Website/API):**
    *   **API for Agents:** Allows agents to connect, authenticate their human-backed identity (via SIWE + AgentBook lookup), and initiate invoice creation. The service verifies the agent's connection to a real World ID Owner.
    *   **Invoice Validation Form:** A publicly accessible interface to check the authenticity and details of any invoice.

4.  **ENS Invoice:**
    *   A unique ENS subdomain created by the Agent (e.g., `invoice01.billie.eth`).
    *   Represents the invoice and stores its key data in ENS Text Records:
        *   **Amount & Currency:** Payment details.
        *   **Status:** Open, Paid, Rejected, etc.
        *   **Recipient:** The root ENS domain registered by the Agent (`billie.eth`).
        *   **Service Signature:** A cryptographic signature from your service, confirming the invoice was generated by a verified human-backed agent via your platform.
        *   **(Optional) Owner's HumanID:** An anonymized identifier of the World ID Owner who delegated authority to the agent.

---

## 🚀 User Flow (Brief)

1.  **Agent Registration:** An agent registers in AgentBook, establishing a link with the Owner's World ID.
2.  **Invoice Creation:**
    *   The agent connects to your API, authenticates their identity (human-backed status + signature), and proves their association with the Owner's World ID.
    *   Upon successful verification, the agent registers a root ENS domain (`billie.eth`) to their wallet (for gas payment).
    *   The agent then initiates the creation of an ENS subdomain (the invoice), recording its details in Text Records, including your service's signature and the Owner's `humanId`.
3.  **Invoice Verification:** Any counterparty can inspect the invoice via your form, viewing its details and on-chain confirmation of authenticity generated by your service.

---

## ✨ Value Proposition

*   **Automation & Delegation:** Agents manage domain registration and invoice issuance, freeing up the Owner's time.
*   **Verification:** Provides on-chain proof that an invoice was initiated by a real individual (via Owner's World ID), executed by an authorized entity (Agent), and validated by your trusted service.
*   **Transparency:** All invoice details are recorded on ENS and available for auditing.
*   **Simplified Architecture:** Eliminates the need for `setApprovalForAll` and direct Owner domain ownership, streamlining onboarding and reducing risks.
